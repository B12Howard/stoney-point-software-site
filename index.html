Apps we've built for manufacturers, interior designers, recruiters and restaurants

  1. 01 — Ironsmith

    Ironsmith's quotes, sales orders and commission reports, in one portal

    The price of a tree grate depends on many parameters. The portal stores Ironsmith's pricing rules once, so staff pick a product and the portal works out the price.

    Read about Ironsmith →

  2. 02 — Kynzo

    LinkedIn commenting for recruiters, connected to the CRMs they already use

    Kynzo pulls posts from the accounts a recruiter is targeting and drafts a comment for each one. It integrates with major recruiting CRMs, including JobAdder, Loxo, Recruit CRM and HubSpot. Built and maintained for the Kynzo team.

    Read about Kynzo →

  3. 03 — Totes

    Your client picks, agrees and pays from one link.

    One link where your client approves products, accepts your terms and pays. Built for interior designers and remodelers, with payments going to your own gateway. It started as an app for one firm, Remodel Showroom.

    Read about Totes →

  4. 04 — CivicZone

    Municipal land use bylaws, searchable section by section

    CivicZone turns a land use bylaw PDF into a website with a table of contents, defined terms that link to their definitions, and search that matches by meaning as well as keywords. We built the demo sites on behalf of CivicZone and Blue Rock Planning.

    Read about CivicZone →

  5. 05 — Hanford Rice Bowl

    Online ordering connected to the restaurant's own point of sale

    A storefront built on your product catalog, with a recommendation engine trained on your customers' order history. The menu comes from the POS, and online orders go back to it for staff to accept or reject.

    Read about Rice Bowl →
