Built for one remodeling firm first

The first version ran only for Remodel Showroom. Their design team exported a Gather project, the client picked items on a vision board and paid at checkout, and the order went to the team's Slack.

Then rebuilt so any firm can run it

Each firm gets its own address, its own logo, colors and terms, and its own staff logins. Card payments run through the firm's own Authorize.net, QuickBooks Payments or Chipd account. Alerts go to Slack, text or email.

What we built

Payments
Authorize.net, QuickBooks Payments or Chipd, through the firm's own account
Built with
Node, TypeScript, React and PostgreSQL. The Remodel Showroom version was Flask and Python.
Our role
Our product. We build it, set up each firm and support it
