The menu comes from the point of sale

The restaurant's point of sale sends menu items, categories and prices to the storefront. Online orders go back to the point of sale, where staff accept or reject them, and the customer gets an email either way.

A storefront built on your product catalog, with a recommendation engine trained on your customers' order history.

What we built

Orders
Pickup, paid at the counter
Owner tools
Sales by hour, menu engineering and basket analysis, with suggested actions for each menu item
Built with
MedusaJS, React, PostgreSQL and Redis
