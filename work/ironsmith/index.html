Ironsmith's quotes, sales orders and commission reports, in one portal

The price of a tree grate depends on many parameters

The portal stores Ironsmith's pricing rules once. Staff pick a product and the portal works out the price, so every quote is priced the same way.

What we built

Reports
Quotes by state and month, sales orders by rep, open purchase orders and custom drawing status, among others
