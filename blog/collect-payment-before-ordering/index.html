How to collect payment from clients before you place the order

Deposits, a payment schedule clients like, and early payment discounts that help a client go from choosing products to paying for them, drawn from the system a remodeling firm in the Hamptons that came up with.

Tote was the idea of a remodeling firm owner in the Hamptons, whose sales and design team help clients choose tile, fixtures and cabinetry every day. Between them, they knew exactly where orders slowed down: in the days after a client had agreed to a selection but before the client had paid for it.

The owner and the team also had a clear picture of what would fix it: a deposit the client could agree to right away, a way to pay part of the total now and the rest before delivery, and a small reward for deciding early. We worked directly with the owner, sales and design team, who told us what they needed and we would iterate on ideas and do tests. What follows is their insight, as we came to understand it while building Tote with them.

Why a client who loved every selection can still hesitate to pay

Clients work with their interior designer and they spend a lot of time working with their client picking specs and creating a design. Then the cost arrives and there is some sticker shock.

The client would like a few days to think it over, or to show their spouse, or to wait until the end of the month. While they wait, you can't order anything and there's no cash flow.

What usually stands between a yes and a payment

Three things tend to get in the way:

  • The client sees the whole amount at once and has no smaller step to take, so they're being asked to commit to the full amount in one go.
  • There's not a lot of reason for a customer to make a decision quickly.
  • Paying has a lot of friction, as the owner of the remodeling firm put it: emails back and forth, a DocuSign request and then a check.

Set a payment schedule: part now, the rest before delivery

On a larger order, asking for the full amount on the day the client approves can be a big ask. It's often easier to ask for a portion now, such as half, and to collect the balance before the products are delivered. You decide which percentage makes sense for your business, and that split becomes a simple payment schedule the client can see before they commit: the deposit already credited, a share of the product total today, and the balance before delivery. Tote keeps any unpaid balance visible on your dashboard and reminds whoever owns the job until the balance is marked paid, so the second payment is never forgotten.

Use an early payment discount to help the client decide this week

An early payment discount, meaning a modest reward for paying promptly, can help incentivize customers to pull the trigger within a week. The owner settled on three discounts for the firm's clients: 5% for paying up front, 2% for moving quickly and 2% for ordering more than one room at a time.

In Tote, each one discount appears on its own line just above the total, so the client can see exactly what saying yes now is worth to them. Every firm can rename the discounts, change the amounts or add a one-off discount for a particular client.

You bring your own terms of service

Every extra step between a client saying yes and a client paying is one more place for the order to stall. With Tote, the client reviews each item, says yes or no to it, agrees to your terms and pays, all in one sitting from their computer or phone. Your terms, the order and the payment all sit on that one page. The deposit, the amount due today and any discounts are set on the tote before you send it, so the client sees the complete picture the first time they open the link.

How Tote can help

Everything in Tote grew out of one home remodel owner's thinking and the day-to-day experience of the sales and design team, and it's in use at the firm today. It's made for remodelers and interior designers who order products on their clients' behalf. Payments run through the gateway you choose, whether that's Authorize.net, QuickBooks Payments or Chipd, (or talk with us to connect another gateway) and go straight into your own merchant account.

We bring on a small number of firms at a time and set each one up personally, so that the gateway, the email and the branding are correct. If getting paid is the part of your business that slows you down, we'd be glad to hear how you handle it today.
