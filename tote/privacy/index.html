Privacy Policy

Remodel Showroom Tote Extension — Effective September 17, 2026

Remodel Showroom Tote Extension (“Extension”, “we”, “our”, “us”) is a Chrome extension that lets Tote staff create purchasable Totes directly from a GatherIt project page, using their own firm's Tote account.

1. Information We Collect

The Extension may collect or process:

  • Account credentials:
    • The Tote account email and password you enter to connect the Extension. Your password is sent once, over HTTPS, to authenticate you, and is never stored by the Extension.
    • The session tokens (a short-lived access token and a longer-lived refresh token) issued after you connect, used to keep you signed in without re-entering your password.
  • Firm/tenant configuration:
    • Your Tote subdomain (which firm's Tote account you're connecting to)
    • An optional custom API base URL, if you set one for local/dev testing (e.g. localhost or an ngrok tunnel)
  • Personally identifiable information (PII):
    • Salesperson name entered by you in Extension settings
    • Data from the intercomSettings object on a Gather project page, when present (used to auto-fill the salesperson name; may include a name, email, or other fields the page exposes)
    • Your GatherIt API key, entered by you in Settings and sent to your firm's Tote backend so it can fetch data from GatherIt; not retained locally after saving
  • Gather project data:
    • Project name and project ID from Gather pages
    • Item IDs and item names for the items you select, and which of those items you mark as customer-selectable
    • Deposit and discount choices you make when creating a Tote
  • Local Tote-creation history:
    • A record of Totes you've created (Tote URL, project ID/name, item IDs/names, timestamps, and the backend's response), kept so you can view your history in the popup
  • Technical/operational data:
    • Extension event logs (up to the 50 most recent entries) for status/error visibility in the popup

The Extension does not read, upload, or transmit spreadsheet or other file contents.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Authenticate you and identify which firm's Tote backend to communicate with
  • Create Totes from the Gather project and item data you select
  • Populate or assist optional fields (such as salesperson name)
  • Let your firm's Tote backend fetch data from GatherIt on your behalf
  • Provide extension status, Tote-creation history, and logs for troubleshooting

3. Where Data Is Stored

  • Locally on your device: The Extension stores settings and operational data using Chrome extension storage and local browser databases (for example, chrome.storage.local and IndexedDB). Your account password is never stored locally in any form.
  • Remotely (when you connect or create a Tote): Login credentials, your GatherIt API key, and Gather project/item data are sent over HTTPS to your firm's own Tote backend, at https://{your-subdomain}.totesyes.com (or a custom URL you explicitly configure for testing). Each firm's Tote backend only receives data from that firm's own users — there is no shared or third-party backend.
  • Read from Gather, not sent back: The Extension reads project and item data from GatherIt pages (go.gatherit.co) you have open, but does not transmit anything back to GatherIt.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data.

Data is shared only with your own firm's Tote backend, as necessary to authenticate you and perform the Tote-creation functionality described above.

5. Data Retention

  • Local extension data remains until cleared by the user, overwritten, or removed by uninstalling the extension. Event logs are automatically capped at the 50 most recent entries.
  • Server-side data retention depends on your firm's Tote backend's own policies and operational requirements.

6. User Controls

You can:

  • Edit or remove Extension settings in the popup
  • Disconnect your account at any time, which clears your stored session tokens
  • Remove individual Tote-creation history entries, or clear all extension-local data via browser extension storage controls
  • Uninstall the Extension to stop all future collection by the Extension

7. Security

We use HTTPS for all network requests. Authentication uses a short-lived access token (refreshed automatically) plus a long-lived, rotating refresh token, rather than storing your password.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect data handled by the Extension.

8. Children's Privacy

The Extension is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When updated, we will revise the “Effective date” above.
