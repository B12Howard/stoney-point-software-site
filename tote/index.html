Tote puts the selections, your terms and the payment on one page, so the client can finish in one sitting.

How a tote works.

Step 1

Build it where you already spec.

Tick the items in your Gather project and click Create Tote. Photos, SKUs, quantities and prices come over as they are, so nobody retypes a spec sheet. Not on Gather? Upload a spreadsheet.

Set the deposit, the amount due today and any discounts before the link goes out.

Step 2

They decide item by item.

Items are grouped by room with photos, quantities and prices. Your client taps Yes or No on each one. No account, no download, works on a phone.

Lock the items the job depends on so they can't be dropped. When the client pays, your team sees what they said no to, so you know what to follow up on.

Step 3

Terms and payment in the same step.

Before they can pay, your client reads your terms and clicks I Agree. Then they pay by card. Your terms print on their receipt.

That replaces the separate signature request and the separate invoice.

Step 4

Everyone knows it's paid.

The client gets a PDF receipt from your email address. Your team gets a Slack message, text or email listing what sold and what didn't.

Every tote shows on one dashboard with its status and salesperson.

Give them a reason to say yes today.

Big orders make clients hesitate. Put the reason to commit on the order itself.

  • 5% off for paying up front, 2% for doing more than one room, 2% for deciding quickly. Change the amounts or add your own.
  • Discounts stack, and each one shows as its own line, so the client sees what they're saving.
  • Credit a deposit you already collected.
  • Charge part of the total now and collect the rest before delivery.

Your gateway. Your accounts.

Card payments run through your own Authorize.net, QuickBooks Payments or Chipd account, at the rate you already pay. The money lands in your merchant account. We don't take a cut.

Receipts go out from your email. Alerts go to your Slack or your phone.

Use a different gateway or messaging tool? Tell us. We add new ones for a firm once they're ready to use them.

After they pay.

  • A tote left unpaid for 5 days sends the salesperson a reminder.
  • If a payment is held for review or doesn't finish, the owner gets an email and the tote is flagged. Held Authorize.net payments are checked every 15 minutes and clear on their own.
  • Part-paid totes stay on the dashboard, with reminders, until someone marks the balance paid.
  • Sent the wrong one? Void it and the link stops working.
  • Export paid and unpaid totes by date range for your bookkeeper.

Questions.

What does Tote cost?

$250/month, flat. Not per seat, not per transaction.

Will my price change later?

Not if you're one of our first 10 clients. That $250/month founding rate is locked in for as long as you're a customer, even as pricing changes for anyone who joins after.

Is there an e-signature?

Your client has to agree to your terms before they can pay, and the terms print on their receipt. There's no separate document to sign and chase.

Whose payment processor handles the transaction?

Yours. Tote works with Authorize.net, QuickBooks Payments and Chipd. Client payments go into your account at your own rate, and we don't add a fee on top. Use something else? We'll add it when you're ready to use it.

Who owns the data?

You do. Payments settle in your merchant account, receipts go out from your email, and you can export your tote report whenever you want.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes. Each staff member gets their own login and manages their own totes. Owners see everything across the firm on one dashboard.

What's involved in setup?

We set it up with you: your gateway, your email, your Slack or text alerts, your logo, colors and terms. Your clients see your name on every tote, not ours.

Why can't I just sign up?

We take on a few firms at a time and set each one up ourselves. That way setup gets done right and we stay close to how you actually work.

Can I edit a tote after I've sent it?

No. A tote is fixed once sent. If something changes, send a new one and void the old one.

How long does a link last?

7 days. After that, send a new one.

Can I keep the link private?

Yes. Add the client's phone number and they'll confirm it before they can see the tote.

Does my client need an account?

No. They open the link on any device and go straight to the selections.
