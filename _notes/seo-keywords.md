# Tote SEO keyword research

Last updated: 2026-09-28. Folder starts with `_` so GitHub Pages (Jekyll) does not publish it.

## How this was done (and its limits)

- **Demand signal:** Google autocomplete (`suggestqueries.google.com`, US, English). A phrase that autocomplete suggests is something people actually type. Autocomplete does **not** give search volume, so "demand" below is relative, not a number.
- **Competition:** what ranks on page one today (web search, Sept 2026). Small blogs and forums = easier. Software vendors, big brands and finance sites = harder.
- **Fit:** does the searcher run a design or remodeling business (our buyer), and does Tote solve what they searched for?
- **Not done:** no paid tool (Ahrefs, Semrush) and no Google Keyword Planner. Before a big bet, check volume in Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account) and re-check page one.

Scores are 1 to 5. Priority = Fit × 2 + Demand + Ease (Ease = 6 − Competition).

## What people add to these searches (autocomplete)

- "interior design deposit": payment schedule, design fee, fees, consultation fee
- "interior design retainer": agreement, fee, example, "what is a design retainer"
- "how much deposit should a contractor": what is a reasonable deposit for a contractor, do contractors require a deposit, how much of a deposit can a contractor ask for
- "how much deposit for": renovation, bathroom remodel, kitchen remodel
- "contractor payment schedule": example, California, template, Reddit
- "early payment discount": example, calculator, accounting treatment, journal entry, formula
- "how to get clients to pay": on time, faster, outstanding invoices, overdue invoice

## Notes and cautions

- **New York Lien Law §71-a** requires a home improvement contractor to deposit payments received before substantial completion into a NY escrow account within 5 business days (or post a bond or letter of credit), and to tell the owner where. Anything we publish about remodeling deposits for NY readers must not contradict this, and a dedicated post needs legal review. Sources: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/LIE/71-A, https://ag.ny.gov/home-improvement-fact-sheet
- Page one for designer payment topics commonly states: design deposit 25 to 50% of the design fee, and 100% of product cost (or 50% with balance before shipping) before ordering. Treat these as what competitors say, not as our advice.
- Blog post `/blog/collect-payment-before-ordering/` (2026-09-28) targets keyword 3 as the main phrase, with 1, 4 and 5 as supporting phrases.
- `tote/seo-research.md` (July 2026) is publicly served at stoneypointsoftware.com/tote/seo-research.md. Its volume and difficulty ratings have no stated source. Consider moving it into `_notes/`.
